Comfort, with more control

A concept robe built around adjustable air chambers, climate controls, and a sleeve-mounted interface.

Inflatable air chambers

Air chambers between fabric layers add adjustable loft. Select a fuller or flatter feel in the concept controls; the chamber design is still in development.

Airflow concept

The design explores small fans and internal channels to move air through the garment. Cooling and noise performance have not been tested.

Open-ear audio concept

A proposed collar audio system aims to keep ears uncovered. The speaker design and sound quality are not finalized.

Ambient lighting concept

Optional low-profile lighting could add a soft ambient glow. Placement, controls, and power needs are still being explored.

Good to know

Practical details for everyday care and use.

How do the inflatable air chambers work?

They are a proposed way to adjust the robe's loft by changing the amount of air held between fabric layers. The chamber layout and inflation method are still being developed.

Can I wash the robe?

Care instructions are not finalized. If the concept advances, removable electronics and a clear cleaning process will be part of the design requirements.

Is the robe available to order?

No. This is an early concept, not a product listing. The page does not accept orders or payments.

What are the battery life and weight?

Neither is confirmed. Both will depend on the final garment, air system, and electronics.
