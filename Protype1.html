Good to know

Practical details for everyday care and use.

Can I wash the robe?

The robe is being designed with removable electronics and battery components. Remove all electronic modules before cleaning; final wash instructions will be provided with the production garment.

How long does the battery last?

The concept includes a 10,000 mAh USB-C power bank. Runtime depends on the selected climate and lighting settings, and has not yet been finalized.

How much does it weigh?

Final product weight is still being determined as the garment and hardware are refined. A verified weight will be shared before production.
